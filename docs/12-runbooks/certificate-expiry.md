# Certificate Expiry

1. Confirm whether the affected artifact is an IOMAD course certificate,
   badge, compliance record, or TLS certificate; they have different owners.
2. For learning recertification, verify course completion, tenant policy,
   warning window, and scheduled-task state. Do not alter issue records
   directly.
3. For TLS, renew through the approved certificate manager and validate the
   complete hostname set before deployment.
4. Notifications must use company branding without exposing certificate codes
   or learner data in logs or chat.
5. Verify pre-expiry warning, expired access behavior, reissue/recertification,
   and unrelated-company isolation.
